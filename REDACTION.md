# Publication and redaction policy

This is a post-event educational archive.

## Kept in the public repository

- Challenge names, points and solve counts.
- Technical methodology, algorithms, offsets and vulnerability classes.
- Self-authored solvers.
- Cryptographic hashes of challenge artifacts where useful for provenance.
- Negative results that prevent future researchers from repeating dead ends.

## Kept private

- Exact CTF flags.
- Account passwords and platform bearer tokens.
- Web3 private keys and session cookies.
- Ephemeral gates, tickets and session tokens.
- Live IP addresses, hostnames, RPC endpoints and ports.
- Raw artifacts not necessary to understand the writeup.

Placeholders such as `<HOST>`, `<PORT>`, `<RPC>`, `<COOKIE>` and `<PRIVATE_KEY>` are intentional.

## How the solve list was verified

A challenge appears as solved only when I found its acceptance evidence in the original event transcript. The 31 solves add up to the final 9600-point score. Incomplete work is listed separately instead of being promoted to a solve.
